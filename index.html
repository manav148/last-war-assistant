1 First-Time Setup

Enter Your Activation Code

When you first open Last War Assistant, you are greeted with the secure Activation screen. The assistant automatically recognizes your phone model and hardware identifier.

  • Get your code: Obtain your personal license key (e.g. VIP-WAR-2026 or TEST-101) from your administrator.
  • Input your code: Type the code into the text field. Codes are case-insensitive for your convenience.
  • Tap "Activate Assistant": The app connects securely to verify your license and bind your device.
Device Limit Protection: Each code allows a specific number of active devices. Once activated, your device remains unlocked automatically every time you launch the app.
2 Permissions

Grant Required Permissions

Once activated, the Main Dashboard displays your active license status. To operate non-intrusively on top of Last War, the assistant requires two standard Android permissions:

  • Display Over Other Apps: Tap GRANT. Find "Last War Assistant" and toggle the switch to Allow. This allows the floating shield bubble to render over your game.
  • Accessibility Service: Tap ENABLE. Select "Last War Assistant" under Downloaded Apps and turn it ON. This allows automated tapping without root or cables.
  • Launch Floating Service: Tap the large teal "START FLOATING ASSISTANT" button to launch the overlay.
Privacy & Safety: The Accessibility Service only interacts with the designated assistant controls and your alliance help actions. It never accesses passwords or personal accounts.
3 In-Game Controls

Using the Floating HUD

Switch to Last War. You will see a small floating shield bubble near the screen edge. Tap it anytime to expand the full tactical control HUD:

  • Help (Auto Alliance Help): Automatically taps the alliance handshake when clan mates request building or research help.
  • Chat Alert: Real-time OCR monitors alliance chat for fireworks, secret tasks, and gift drops—alerting you with distinctive sound & vibration.
  • Live Statistics: Keeps count of your total Alliance Helps and Chat Alerts triggered in real-time.
  • Start / Stop: Tap START to engage the active loops or STOP to pause automation without closing the HUD.
Drag Anywhere: You can drag and drop the floating shield bubble to any part of your screen so it never obstructs your game buttons or troops.

Peace Shield & Custom Alarms

Never let your base shield drop unexpectedly or miss critical server event deadlines:

6 Device Management

Switching or Upgrading Devices

Each license code comes with an allocated device slot. If you purchase a new phone or switch devices, you can unbind your license in seconds:

  • Open Last War Assistant: Go to the main dashboard on your current device.
  • Tap "Deactivate": Located in the upper right of the ACTIVE LICENSE card.
  • Confirm Unlink: The app frees up your device slot. You can now enter the same code on your new device immediately!
Need more devices simultaneously? If you want to run the assistant on multiple phones simultaneously, ask your administrator to increase the device limit on your code.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where do I get an Activation Code?

Activation codes are issued directly by your administrator or alliance leader. Contact them to receive your unique code.

What if I see "Device limit reached"?

Each code is configured with a maximum number of concurrent devices. Either tap Deactivate on your old device to free the slot, or ask your administrator to increase your allowed devices.

Why did the floating bubble disappear?

Some Android phone manufacturers (Xiaomi, Samsung, OnePlus) have aggressive battery savers. Set "Battery Usage" for Last War Assistant to Unrestricted so the system doesn't close it in the background.

Does this require Root or PC tethering?

No. Last War Assistant runs completely standalone on your phone utilizing official Android Accessibility and System Alert Window APIs.

Can I move the floating shield bubble?

Yes. Simply press and hold the shield icon to drag it to any side or corner of your screen so it never covers your buttons.

Do reminders work when the screen is locked?

Yes. Reminders use Android's high-precision alarm system to wake your device and sound an alert even when the screen is turned off.
