### 🚀 Quick Start
1. Download and install `LastWarAssistant-v2.0.0.apk` on your Android device.
2. Launch the app and enter your personal **Activation Code** provided by your administrator.
3. Grant **Display Over Other Apps** and **Accessibility Service** permissions.
4. Set your in-game language in **Last War: Survival** to **English** (Avatar &rarr; Settings ⚙️ &rarr; Language &rarr; English).
5. Tap **Start Floating Assistant** and open Last War!

### 📖 Features
- **Auto Alliance Help**: Continuous background assistance for clan buildings and research.
- **Chat Alert Radar**: Instant chime & vibration alerts when fireworks, secret tasks, or gift chests appear in chat.
- **Peace Shield & Event Alarms**: High-priority countdown timers and 8-hour shield presets with exact alarms.
- **Chat Extractor**: One-tap copy of 25 to 200 alliance messages straight to clipboard.
- **In-Game Sticky Notes**: Scratchpad for coordinates, war targets, and tactical reminders.
